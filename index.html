Sources & methods

Air quality

DOE / DOSM: national monthly concentrations, 2018–2024. WHO SDGPM25: annual modelled exposure, 2017–2023. Open-Meteo / CAMS: location-based model estimates. Hourly estimates become daily, monthly and annual averages.

Weather & hotspots

Open-Meteo historical weather: seven coordinate-matched locations. The export does not identify its exact weather-model selection. NASA FIRMS S-NPP: archived satellite detections. Retained records use type 0 and nominal/high confidence. Dates use Malaysia time, converted from UTC.

Boundaries & limits

geoBoundaries / OpenStreetMap / Wambacher: 2017 ADM1 boundaries, ODbL 1.0. Main comparisons use complete years 2024–2025. One unconfirmed location is excluded. 69 hotspots lack a state match. NASA documents a historical fire-type classification issue. This export cannot verify its processing revision.

Read the preparation notes and chart inventory

Twelve charts include eight advanced idioms. Four advanced idioms are maps. Others include slope, heatmap, jitter and radial bar charts. Missing readings remain missing. Zero-detection days indicate no retained detections. They cannot establish an absence of fire.

Project purpose and design framework

Domain: Malaysian air quality, weather and fire hotspots.

Audience: Malaysian readers exploring pollution patterns.

What: Concentrations, rainfall, dates, locations and satellite detections.

Why: Compare places, identify changes and investigate possible associations.

How: Maps locate patterns; temporal charts reveal changes. Distribution charts expose variation hidden by averages. Shared filters support comparisons across related charts.

Main comparisons use the latest complete overlapping years available. Partial 2026 records are excluded. The national catalogue confirms coverage through December 2024. Other providers’ release dates still require verification.
